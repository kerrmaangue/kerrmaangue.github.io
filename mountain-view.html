Background

The campus program combines the New Public Safety Building and the Parking Garage for the City of Mountain View. Running two structures under one program means two sets of consultants, submittals and permits that have to stay aligned.

Scope & deliverables

Responsibilities

  • Dual-structure coordination across the building and the garage
  • Master RFI and submittal register control
  • A/E selection schedule tracking
  • Multi-agency permit logging
  • Inter-agency communication directory
  • Weekly status reporting for city stakeholders

Key deliverables

  • $100M+ Program Master Submittal Log
  • RFI Register with owner, due date and days open
  • Pre-Con Look-Ahead Schedule

Core takeaways

  • One control system for both structures, so the city team works from a single, current picture
  • Master submittal log, RFI register and pre-con look-ahead schedule built and maintained
  • Weekly status reports kept city stakeholders informed of what is done and what is waiting
