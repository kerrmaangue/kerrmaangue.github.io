About

Scope of services

I'm the person project teams bring in to keep the paper trail straight — pulling permit histories, verifying parcel and title data, cleaning up a CRM nobody's touched since the last PM left, or running the inbox and calendar behind a busy principal.

I work with government agencies, general contractors and construction companies, and architecture, engineering, and real estate development firms — usually stepping in wherever a project's information has outgrown the person tracking it.

Research & data collection

Permit and zoning research, parcel and title lookups, market surveys, and regulatory reference work compiled into a usable, cited record.

Data entry & CRM management

Contact and contractor databases, bid and subcontractor tracking, pipeline hygiene, and migrating spreadsheet chaos into a CRM the team will use.

Virtual assistance & admin support

Scheduling, correspondence, submittal and RFI logs, meeting minutes, and the recurring administrative load of a project office.
