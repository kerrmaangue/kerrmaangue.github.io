Schedule, Risk & Compliance Tracking

Milestones, risks and approvals in one place, so slips show up early.

For: General Contractors, Owners, AEC Firms

Pre-construction workflow

  1. Capture the master schedule and key milestones.
  2. Set up permit, RFI and submittal logs.
  3. Update statuses weekly and flag approaching dates.
  4. Maintain the risk register with owners and actions.
  5. Issue a short look-ahead before each coordination meeting.

Pain points solved

  • Permit dates that sneak up on the team
  • RFIs sitting unanswered
  • Submittals approved late
  • Risks discussed once and never tracked

Logs and templates I maintain

  • Permit log
  • RFI log
  • Submittal matrix
  • Risk register
