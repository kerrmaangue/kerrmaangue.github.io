Program overview

About the program

The City of Mountain View is replacing its existing 1980-built police and fire headquarters at 1000 Villa Street with a new public safety building and multi-level parking garage — a capital program valued in the hundreds of millions of dollars. The project's Environmental Impact Report was certified in November 2025, with detailed design continuing into 2026 and construction phased over several years.

My involvement was limited to tracking RFP and RFQ submissions during the program's consultant/vendor solicitation process in 2025 — I was not involved in design, construction, or budget management for this program.

Public program sources This program is public record. For official project details, status, and budget, see the City of Mountain View's capital projects page and Mountain View Voice coverage of the program's funding plan.
