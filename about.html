Scope of services

Who I work with, where the research background comes from, and the three ways I typically get looped into a project.

Open to full-time roles Open to project engagements

I'm the person project teams bring in to keep the paper trail straight — pulling permit histories, verifying parcel and title data, cleaning up a CRM nobody's touched since the last PM left, or running the inbox and calendar behind a busy principal.

I work with government agencies, general contractors and construction companies, and architecture, engineering, and real estate development firms — usually stepping in wherever a project's information has outgrown the person tracking it.

Before going independent, I spent five years as a National Planning Team Reporter for Dodge Construction Network, tracking permits, zoning, and planning activity nationwide — the research foundation this practice is built on.
