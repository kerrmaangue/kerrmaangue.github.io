Budget, Invoice & Vendor Coordination

Invoices, vendor files and budget numbers kept current and easy to review.

For: General Contractors, Owners, AEC Firms

Pre-construction workflow

  1. Set up the budget tracker from your cost codes.
  2. Collect and file vendor documents.
  3. Log each invoice and its approval status.
  4. Reconcile invoices against the budget monthly.
  5. Send a summary of spend, open items and next payments.

Pain points solved

  • Invoices lost in email
  • Vendor paperwork incomplete
  • Budget numbers out of date
  • Unclear what is approved and what is paid

Logs and templates I maintain

  • Invoice log
  • Vendor file checklist
  • Budget-to-actual summary
  • Payment status report
