Project background

The campus program combines the New Public Safety Building and the Parking Garage at 1000 Villa St for the City of Mountain View.

Running two structures under one program means two sets of consultants, submittals and permits that have to stay aligned. One control system for both keeps the city team working from a single, current picture.
