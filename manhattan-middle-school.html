Manhattan Middle School Improvements

Boulder Valley School District RE-2 · Colorado — a multi-scope campus renovation spanning building systems, accessibility, interior upgrades, and a dedicated Career & Technical Education renovation.

Role

Remote Project Controls & Administrative Coordination — maintaining schedules, risk logs, approvals, documentation, and stakeholder communications.

Worked alongside

Supported the project's construction management team and district facilities stakeholders through coordination and documentation.

Scope of involvement

End-to-end coordination support from initial planning through active construction tracking and closeout documentation.

Project scope

What the project covered

Building Systems

Plumbing system component repairs and replacements to prevent system failure, plus full scoreboard replacement.

Infrastructure & Site

Concrete repairs and site restoration across affected campus areas.

Safety & Code Compliance

Targeted ADA accessibility updates across building entry points and facilities.

Interior Space Improvements

Interior finish upgrades including painting and flooring, full student locker replacement, and select restroom renovations.

Career & Technical Education (CTE)

A dedicated $455,000 renovation of the CTE learning environment, coordinated from planning through budget tracking and completion.
