Project background

Improvements to Manhattan Middle School cover building systems, plumbing and ADA work, plus a $455k career and technical education (CTE) renovation for Boulder Valley School District RE-2.

A renovation like this runs many small, connected items at once: trade sequencing, vendor submittals, repair records, accessibility fixes and a budget that has to stay matched to the CTE scope. My job was to keep those records current so the project team could see status without chasing it.
